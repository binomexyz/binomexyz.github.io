Background

Contazara is a technology company founded in Zaragoza in 1984, specialising in the development of smart measurement and management equipment. Its experience, capacity for innovation and commitment to sustainability have made it a leading company in the water sector.

The project also coincided with a new stage of growth and diversification, marked by the expansion of its offer into gas measurement.

The challenge

Contazara needed to update an identity that no longer reflected the size, innovation or experience the company had built up. Its digital communication gave a fragmented image, with an outdated website and a social media presence lacking a coherent, recognisable visual and editorial system suited to its new stage.

Our approach

We created an identity that expresses technology, precision and experience without losing the brand’s industrial character. We designed a new website to organise its range of products and services, and developed a visual and editorial system for social media that turns its technical knowledge into clear, useful and recognisable content.
