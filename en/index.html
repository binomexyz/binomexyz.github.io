Frequently asked questions

What does Binōme do?

Binōme is a boutique digital studio in Madrid run by two designers. We do branding, websites, digital product (UX/UI) and digital strategy for companies that want to deliver the same quality online as they do in person.

Who will I be working with?

Directly with the two partners, Álvaro Fabre (design and web) and Betsabé Meneses (branding and UX), with no intermediaries, from the first brief to the final result.

How much does a website or a brand identity cost?

It depends on scope, content and functionality. Tell us about your project and we will reply within 24 hours with a tailored proposal.

Do you keep working with me once the site is live?

Yes. We support clients through the first months after launch to answer questions, fine-tune and make sure the project performs as we expect.

Do you work in other languages?

We work in Spanish, French and English.
